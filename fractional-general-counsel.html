Fractional General Counsel

Ongoing Legal Support That is Flexible by Design

A Fractional General Counsel provides businesses with ongoing access to experienced legal leadership through a flexible engagement model. Instead of seeking legal support only when specific issues arise, businesses benefit from a continuing legal relationship that remains connected to their operations, priorities, and changing requirements.

The FGC Model

Legal Support That Fits the Way Your Business Works

The Fractional General Counsel model is built around ongoing access, continuity, and flexibility. It can supplement an existing legal team or provide comprehensive legal support where none currently exists.

01

Where No In-House Legal Function Exists

For businesses without an internal legal team, the Fractional General Counsel provides a continuing legal relationship that covers day-to-day legal queries, commercial matters, governance, compliance, and strategic decisions as they arise — without the fixed cost of a full-time appointment.

02

Alongside an Existing In-House Legal Team

For businesses with an existing legal team, the model provides additional senior-level legal capacity — particularly useful during periods of increased workload, significant transactions, strategic initiatives, or organisational change.

03

Tailored Scope and Continuity

The scope of support is agreed based on the business's requirements and can be adjusted as priorities evolve. The engagement is ongoing, not transactional — creating a consistent legal relationship that develops over time and aligns with the business's strategic direction.

04

Accessibility and Responsiveness

Leadership and management can access legal guidance as and when needed — for immediate queries, important decisions, or complex matters — without having to instruct external lawyers on each occasion or wait for a full-time appointment to be in place.

How We Work

A Practical Approach to Ongoing Legal Support

Stage 01  /  05

UNDERSTAND

Understand

Establishing the Legal Landscape of Your Business

Establishing a clear understanding of your business, its objectives, operations, legal environment, and priorities. This foundation allows legal advice to be grounded in the realities of how the business operates and what it is working towards.

Prioritise

Identifying What Matters Most

Identifying the matters requiring immediate attention and the areas where continuing legal support can provide the greatest value. Not every legal issue carries equal weight — knowing where to focus legal effort is essential to effective ongoing counsel.

Advise

Practical Guidance Informed by Context

Providing practical legal guidance informed by the circumstances and objectives of each matter. Legal advice is most useful when it is clear, commercially aware, and shaped by an understanding of the business and the decisions that leadership needs to make.

Support

Working Alongside Your Leadership

Working with business leaders, management teams, in-house legal teams, external law firms, and other stakeholders to help address legal matters and implement decisions. Legal support is most effective when it operates as part of, not separate from, the business.

Adapt

Evolving With Your Business

Adjusting the nature and level of support as your priorities, resources, and requirements evolve. As the business grows and changes, the engagement evolves with it — ensuring that legal support remains aligned with what the business needs at each stage of its development.

Why Continuity Matters

Legal Advice That Helps Businesses Grow

An ongoing legal relationship changes the nature of the advice — it becomes more contextual, more responsive, and more aligned with the business.

When the same counsel works with a business consistently, they spend less time understanding the background of each matter and more time providing relevant, practical guidance. They understand the business's history, its relationships, its risk profile, and what is important to its leadership — and apply that context to every matter they advise on.

Consistent Legal Relationship

Legal support that builds over time, developing familiarity with the business and its priorities.

Responsive Advice

Faster, more informed responses because the counsel already understands the business context.

Support Through Growth

Legal guidance that evolves alongside the business as it expands, changes direction, or enters new markets.

Alongside In-House Teams

Works collaboratively with internal legal functions, adding senior-level capacity without disrupting existing structures.
