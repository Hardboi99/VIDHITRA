Who We Are

Fractional General Counsel for the Business You Are Building

We work alongside your leadership team, providing ongoing legal counsel as your business grows, evolves, and makes decisions that shape its future.

As businesses grow, legal considerations become part of everyday decision-making and not just individual transactions or disputes. Contracts, compliance, people, technology, expansion, and investments all require timely legal input. Without ongoing legal guidance, routine decisions can create unnecessary risk, slow business progress, or result in costly issues later.

Our Fractional General Counsel model provides ongoing access to experienced legal counsel which is integrated with your business. We work alongside founders, promoters, boards, leadership teams, and in-house legal providing practical guidance across corporate law, commercial contracts, governance, regulatory compliance, risk, and strategic business matters.

Rather than stepping in only when a legal issue arises, we work alongside your leadership team bringing continuity, context, and legal support aligned with how your business operates.

The Vidhitra Approach

Legal Counsel Built Around Your Business

Effective legal counsel begins with understanding the business behind the legal issue. We bring business context, continuity, practical judgment, and the flexibility to respond as priorities change.

1

We start with the business, not just the legal question

The legal issue is rarely the whole issue. We first understand what the business is trying to achieve, what is changing, and what the decision needs to accomplish. We then bring the legal perspective into that context.

2

We build context that compounds over time

With ongoing counsel, each matter is informed by what came before. Over time, we develop a deeper understanding of your business, its priorities, operating environment, and how decisions are made. That context reduces repetition and makes our counsel more relevant, practical, and commercially informed.

3

We help leadership weigh the decision, not just the risk

Legal advice is most valuable when it helps leadership evaluate the available paths, not simply identify risk. We help clarify what matters, where the exposure lies, what can be negotiated, and the practical steps that can move the business forward.

4

We provide continuity as your business evolves

As your business grows and its priorities evolve, so does the legal support it requires. Our Fractional General Counsel model provides ongoing access to experienced legal counsel, providing continuity while adapting to your evolving business and priorities.

FAQ

Legal Counsel Built Around Your Business

01

A Different Model for Ongoing Legal Counsel

An ongoing legal relationship that brings continuity, context, and senior-level legal support to businesses as they grow, change, and evolve.
02

What is a Fractional General Counsel?

A Fractional General Counsel is an ongoing legal counsel arrangement that gives a business access to senior-level legal advice and strategic support without appointing a full-time General Counsel. The counsel works with the business on a continuing basis, develops an understanding of its operations and priorities, and provides legal support as needs arise—without becoming part of the organisation’s payroll or adding the overhead associated with a full-time role.
03

How does the Fractional General Counsel model work?

The relationship is ongoing, but the engagement is flexible. Rather than employing a full-time General Counsel, the business retains access to senior legal counsel who understands its context and provides support across its evolving legal needs. The business pays for the legal services and support actually required, based on the agreed engagement structure.
04

What value does a Fractional General Counsel bring to a business?

The model provides continuity without the fixed commitment of a full-time legal leadership role. Over time, the counsel develops an understanding of the business, allowing legal advice to be more informed by its operations, priorities, and objectives. This gives the business access to ongoing senior legal support while keeping the engagement aligned with its actual legal needs.
05

Who can benefit from a Fractional General Counsel?

The model can support founders, startups, SMEs, growth-stage and established businesses, international companies entering or expanding in India, and in-house legal teams that need additional senior-level legal capacity. It is particularly relevant for businesses that need an ongoing legal relationship and access to experienced counsel, but do not require or wish to add a full-time General Counsel to their organisation.
06

When should a business consider a Fractional General Counsel?

A business can engage a Fractional General Counsel from incorporation and at any stage of its growth. The support may range from addressing day-to-day legal queries and advising on important business communications to establishing legal and governance foundations, entering into commercial relationships, raising investment, expanding operations, entering new markets, managing organisational change, and navigating complex business decisions. The engagement evolves with the business, providing ongoing legal counsel aligned with its changing needs and priorities.
07

What role does a Fractional General Counsel play in a business?

A Fractional General Counsel provides ongoing legal counsel that develops with the business. By understanding the organisation, its operations, priorities, and history, the counsel can support day-to-day legal queries, business communications, ongoing legal matters, and important business decisions with greater context. The model can also work alongside external law firms and in-house legal teams, providing additional senior-level capacity, business context, and continuity of legal support as and when required.
